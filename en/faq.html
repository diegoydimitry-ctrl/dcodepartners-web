Everything you usually ask us before getting started.

General

What exactly does D-Code Partners do?

We design and implement custom automation and artificial intelligence systems for small and medium-sized businesses: from repetitive internal processes to agents that serve your customers. All within a working model you can learn about in The D-Code Method.

What sets you apart from a traditional agency?

Most agencies sell a closed project upfront. We read how your company works first, and agree the scope with you — what gets built and what gets measured — before we build anything. You can see the details in Guarantees & Process.

What kind of companies do you work with?

Small and medium-sized businesses with manual, repetitive processes, or that receive a volume of customer inquiries they struggle to handle on time today. On the first call, we assess whether your case is a good fit.

Automation

What processes can you automate?

Document management, reporting, sales follow-up, invoicing, client onboarding, and generally any repetitive task with clear rules. You'll find concrete examples in Automation AI.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

No. We handle all the technical design and implementation. You just need to tell us how your business runs today.

Do I need to change my current software?

Usually not. We design our systems to integrate with the tools you already use — CRM, spreadsheets, ERP, or your website — instead of replacing them. You can see examples in Integrations.

What tools do you use?

We choose the most suitable technology for each specific case: it could be an automation platform, a direct API integration, or custom development. We don't force the same stack on every client.

Do you also build or improve websites?

Yes. If your company doesn't have a website, or the current one isn't up to par, we design or improve it ourselves, built from the ground up to connect with the rest of your system — CRM, automations, and AI agents included. We handle social media management and other channels the same way: through AI automation, not manual content creation.

Artificial Intelligence

What's the difference between an AI agent and a traditional chatbot?

A traditional chatbot follows a fixed script of predefined responses. An AI agent understands natural language, keeps track of the conversation's context, and knows when to hand it off to a person. We explain it in more detail in AI Agents.

Can you integrate AI with my CRM or ERP?

Yes. It's one of the most common combinations: an agent or automated process that logs every interaction directly into your CRM or ERP, with nothing copied by hand. More information in Integrations.

Project & Pricing

What does the process look like from the first meeting?

We start with a diagnosis of your operations, design the system and agree the scope. Then we build it, leave it running inside your tools and review the results together. The full details are in The D-Code Method.

How long does it take to implement an automation system?

The diagnosis happens in the first few days. Implementation is progressive and supervised, and how long it takes depends on the scope we agreed.

How do you measure results?

With concrete data: hours saved, processes automated, and response times, compared against your situation before we started. What gets measured is decided with you before anything is built.

What if the system does not deliver what we expected?

We review it with you against what we agreed to measure, and adjust it. And there is no lock-in forcing you to continue.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the system and the scope. We agree it with you before we start building, not afterwards.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There is no minimum term and no cancellation penalty.

Who owns the automations?

You do. The systems we implement in your operations belong to you; our role is to design them, build them, and leave them running correctly.

What happens after the project is delivered?

We stay available for adjustments and system maintenance. Our model is designed as an ongoing relationship, not a one-off delivery that gets forgotten the next day.

Security & Confidentiality

Is my data, and my customers' data, secure?

Yes. We only access what's strictly necessary for the specific process we're automating, under absolute confidentiality. You can review the details in our Privacy Policy.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)?

Yes, if your company requires it. It's common in projects that involve access to sensitive client data or internal processes.
