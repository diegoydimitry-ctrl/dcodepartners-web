Tax register · VERI*FACTU

Ready for VERI*FACTU

Every invoice, recorded and chained from the moment it is issued.

D-Code Finance keeps a chained, unalterable invoicing record from the moment of issue: every invoice produces its record, with a fingerprint linked to the previous one, and every cancellation leaves its own. The next step is submission to the AEAT. When it has been proven, this page will say so.

  1. 01Ready for VERI*FACTURecord, fingerprint and chain for every invoice, inside the system.Current state
  2. 02VERI*FACTU integration in validationRecords sent to the AEAT test environment, with their responses checked.Next
  3. 03VERI*FACTU integratedSubmission validated end to end against the official specification.Later
  4. 04VERI*FACTU operationalLive submission in production and the responsible declaration signed.Later

What it already does

  • An issue record when each invoice is issued, and a cancellation record when it is cancelled.
  • Chained fingerprint: every record keeps the previous one’s, in a single chain per issuing company.
  • No silent gaps: if the record cannot be chained, the invoice is not issued.
  • Chain check on screen: gaps, duplicates, forks and broken links.
  • System event log (art. 9.1 of RD 1007/2023), chained together.

What comes with the next steps

  • Submission of every record to the AEAT, first in its test environment.
  • The record’s fingerprint and format, checked against the official specification.
  • The verification QR code, printed on every invoice.
  • Live submission in production and the system’s responsible declaration, signed.

Adaptation deadline Companies paying Corporate Tax, before 1 January 2027; everyone else, before 1 July 2027. Source: AEAT information note on Royal Decree-Law 15/2025 of 2 December (checked 21/09/2026).

This state is not typed by hand: it comes from a record that only changes when the D-Code Finance team provides the proof for each step. Last reviewed: 21/09/2026.

The next piece

Bank reconciliation · Coming soon

Your bank statement, matched against what you already have.

Today, knowing what has really reached the bank means going through the statement line by line against the invoices. Reconciliation will do that matching inside D-Code Finance, with the clients, invoices and expenses it already holds, and leave in view only what does not match.

  • You will upload the bank statement as a file.
  • Every incoming payment will be matched to its invoice, and every charge to its expense.
  • Whatever does not match will stay flagged for you to look at, instead of being assigned by eye.
  • And Ask Finance will be able to tell you which movements are still unidentified.

It is not in the system yet: it is the next piece of D-Code Finance. When its state changes, this page will say so, and so will the plans.
