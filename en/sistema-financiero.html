The surface of the system

Fourteen pieces that already work together.

It is not an invoice screen with charts around it: it is the full money circuit of a company, built piece by piece.

InvoicingSeries and sequential numbering, credit notes linked to the original, and a PDF the system generates itself.
CollectionsDebt ageing by bracket, reminders that escalate on their own, and partial payments against one invoice.
ExpensesCaptured from a document with human review, plus categories, suppliers and budget control.
PayablesDue dates, upcoming payments and the state of every expense until it is settled.
QuotesFrom accepted quote to invoice, tracking what is still pending and never billing the same line twice.
Orders & notesConversion between documents while keeping what remains outstanding on each line.
ProjectsReal profitability per project, accrual and cash, not just what was invoiced.
TreasuryPeriod balance with expected receipts and expected payments on the same timeline.
RecurringTemplates for periodic invoicing and control of which periods have already been issued.
RadarExplainable anomaly detection: new expenses, vanished ones, amounts out of range, concentration.
DocumentsUploads and downloads mediated by the application, with every access logged.
QuestionsPlain-language questions about the data, answered by citing where each figure comes from.
SearchOne search box across invoices, expenses, clients, suppliers, quotes and projects.
ActivityA history of what happened on each invoice and each expense, with who and when.
