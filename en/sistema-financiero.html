Technical sheet
Tax register
VERI*FACTU. Every invoice is chained to the previous one with its own fingerprint, and there is a screen where you can check the chain is whole and what has been left out.
Permissions
Seven roles — Owner, Administrator, Management, Finance, Operations, Read only and Superadmin — with permission per module and per action.
Isolation
Each company in its own space, enforced in the same layer that reads the database: a query cannot leave it, not even through a programming mistake.
Trace
Who did what, when and on which record. You look at it from the invoice or the expense itself.
Access
There is no public sign-up: we create the account and each person is invited. A suspended account stops getting in immediately.
Database
PostgreSQL. The data is yours and comes out as CSV whenever you want.

Technical sheet
Formats it reads
PDF with text, scanned PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, CSV and XLSX.
How it files them
It pulls out each record with its origin — which page, which line — and leaves it on the screen where it belongs, as a draft. You confirm. Nothing is saved until you do.
What it does with the doubtful
What it cannot read it does not make up: it flags it and sets it aside for you. A statement with three movements that do not match comes out with those three marked, not spread around by eye.
Traceability
Every answer cites its sources and the queries it was calculated with. No figure without an origin.

Does not include fields or flows specific to your business. Connections go through the API, and building them is quoted separately.
