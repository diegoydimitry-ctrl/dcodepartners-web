Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

This document is new relative to the site's previous legal documentation. It is being added because, in several of its services (for example, CRM automation, lead management, or the client's own customer support), D-Code Partners not only processes its own data as a controller, but may also process personal data belonging to third parties (the client's own clients, leads, or users) on the client's behalf. In that case, D-Code Partners acts as the data processor and the client as the data controller, and Article 28 GDPR requires that relationship to be governed by a contract or other legal act. This page publishes the terms D-Code Partners applies in that role, so that any client can review them before contracting.

Nature of this document: this text is a model set of terms. The data processing relationship with each specific client is formalized through express acceptance of these terms (or an equivalent specific annex) as part of the services contract, not automatically by merely visiting this page.

1. Purpose

This Agreement governs the processing of personal data that D-Code Partners (data processor) carries out on behalf of the client (data controller) in connection with the contracted automation services, in accordance with Article 28 GDPR.

2. Subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of the processing

The processing will cover the personal data that the client enters or allows D-Code Partners' automations to process (for example, contact data of the client's own leads or clients managed through a CRM, form, or automated workflow configured by D-Code Partners). The duration will coincide with that of the services contract; the purpose will be exclusively the performance of the contracted automation service.

3. Instructions from the controller

D-Code Partners will process personal data solely on documented instructions from the client (as reflected in the service proposal, the agreed project configuration, or subsequent written instructions), and will not process it for its own purposes or for purposes other than those agreed.

4. Confidentiality

D-Code Partners guarantees that the individuals authorized to process personal data (currently, Diego Siñeriz and Dimitry Sosenko) have committed to observing confidentiality or are subject to a contractual duty of confidentiality.

5. Sub-processors

To provide the service, D-Code Partners may rely on sub-processors, which are the same technology providers described in the Privacy Policy (among others, depending on the service contracted: n8n Cloud, Airtable, Google Workspace, Vercel). The client grants general authorization for the use of these sub-processors, and D-Code Partners will inform the client of any planned change involving the addition or replacement of sub-processors, giving the client the opportunity to object on justified grounds.

6. Security measures

D-Code Partners will apply the technical and organizational measures described in its Information Security Policy, appropriate to the risk of the specific processing.

7. Assistance to the controller

D-Code Partners will assist the client, to the extent possible and within the reasonable scope of a provider of its size, in complying with its obligations to respond to requests for the exercise of data subject rights and to notify security breaches, when such requests or incidents affect data processed as part of the service.

8. Notification of security breaches

D-Code Partners will notify the client, without undue delay, of any breach of the security of personal data processed on the client's behalf of which it becomes aware, so that the client can comply with its own notification obligations under Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

9. Fate of the data at the end of the service

At the end of the service, D-Code Partners will, at the client's choice, return or delete the personal data processed on its behalf, together with any existing copies, unless applicable law requires their retention.

10. Audit

D-Code Partners will make available to the client the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with these terms. For operational reasons, any on-site audit or inspection will be coordinated and limited to what is strictly necessary, subject to prior agreement between the parties on scope, cost, and timing.

11. Formalization

For clients requiring a specific signed document (for example, an independent DPA annex with their own identifying details and signature), D-Code Partners will formalize it as part of the contracting process. You can request it by writing to dcodedepartment@gmail.com.
