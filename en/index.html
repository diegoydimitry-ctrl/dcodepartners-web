Before we talk about systems

This is an ordinary week. What is underlined is not work.

On Monday somebody opens the inbox and copies five orders into a spreadsheet. On Tuesday a quote comes in and sits there waiting for someone to remember. On Wednesday a client asks about their order and three programs have to be opened to answer. On Thursday an invoice goes out and nobody knows whether the last one was paid. On Friday something has to be decided and the number is asked for by message. The following Monday it all starts again exactly the same.

What the underlines mean

None of those six things is the work you are paid for. And all six are fixed the same way: by putting a system where somebody's memory is now.

Our own software · try it now

Write what you need to know. It answers with your numbers.

D-Code Finance is our finance system. Invoices, collections, expenses, treasury, projects and documents in the same place, and on top of them something you write to in plain language: who owes me, what is overdue, what should I look at today. It answers with the number, shows you where it came from and takes you to the invoice. You do not have to learn thirty screens to find one thing out.

And documents come in through there. You upload the supplier invoice as a PDF and the fields are detected on their own: each one shows the fragment of the document it came from, you review and confirm, and nothing is saved until then. If the PDF is a scan, it says so and stores the document anyway. You have it running below, under Documents.

Three ways to have it

All three are the same system. What changes is how much of it works for you, and how much of it bends to the way you work.

01

Finance

The finance system running on your data, with its standard setup.

  • Day to day: invoices, collections, ready to invoice, expenses, payments, duplicates and documents.
  • The business: quotes, orders, delivery notes, clients, suppliers and projects.
  • Treasury at 30, 60 and 90 days, base or cautious scenario.
  • Import of the clients, invoices and expenses you already have.
  • Your invoice series, your taxes and your payment terms.
  • VAT books as CSV, issued and received, for your accountant.
  • Roles and permissions per person.

Does not include fields or flows specific to your business, or connections to other tools.

02

Finance with intelligence

All of the above and, on top, something that reads your data, answers you and reads your documents.

  • Ask Finance: questions in plain language. Every answer carries the conclusion, the data behind it and what you would check.
  • Every figure shows where it came from and which query produced it.
  • Documents in the conversation: attach a PDF, an Excel, a CSV or an image and it tells you what it contains and how it fits your data.
  • Expense from a PDF: upload the supplier invoice, the fields are detected and each one shows the fragment it came from. You review and confirm; nothing is saved until then.
  • Radar: overdue amounts, price rises above the threshold and supplier concentration. Written, reproducible rules — not an opaque score.
  • Targets: a metric, a period and a floor, and the system tells you whether you are on time.

Does not include custom development on the system.

03

Finance, built to fit

The system, the intelligence, and whatever your company does that nobody else does the same way.

  • Fields, states and flows specific to your operation.
  • Automations built around Finance with the tools you already use.
  • Panels and reports made for your decisions, not generic ones.
  • It evolves: when the company changes, the system changes with it.
  • Half an hour a month to look at what the system records and tune it together.

What we will not promise: what can be connected to what you already use is looked at case by case, and if something cannot be done we say so before we start.

All three are put in the same way: your data is brought in, it gets configured and it is put to work. The price depends on the size of your operation and on what has to be built, and it is agreed in writing before anything starts.

How we work

We read your company, find where the money leaks out and build the system that stops it.

01

We analyse

We follow the real path of a customer, an order and an invoice, end to end, and write down every point where a person has to bridge a gap. Out of that comes the list of where the time, the sales and the money leak out — and how many times a month each one happens.

You leave with that list whether we build it or not
02

We design

We take the heaviest thing on that list and decide what goes with what: what can run on its own, what a person keeps deciding, and which of your tools it connects to. What already works stays as it is, and the rules of your business are set by you.

You see what you will get before it exists
03

We put it in

We build it, connect it to what you already use and leave it running inside the daily job, piece by piece and with your team in it. We do not hand over a manual for somebody to put into practice: we hand over something already in practice.

It runs in your operation, not in a demo
04

We measure and improve

The system leaves a trace of everything it does: what ran on its own, what failed, and where a person is still bridging a gap. That is what it gets corrected with — and when the company changes, the system changes with it instead of going stale.

This is what we get asked to fix

If any of this sounds familiar, there is a system for it.

Opportunities come in and nobody follows them up in timeWe put in a system that collects contacts from the site, the inbox and your ads, ranks them by interest and starts the follow-up on its own, with a warning when one has been sitting for days. Getting paid depends on someone rememberingWe put in the invoices, due dates and collections system: invoices go out when they should, reminders send themselves and a panel says who owes what and since when. Clients wait too long for someone to replyWe put in an agent that answers on the site and by email with the real information about your business, sorts what comes in and passes on to a person what needs a person.
