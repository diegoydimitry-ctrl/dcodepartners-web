How we work

We look at your company, find where the money leaks out, and build the system that stops it.

01

We analyse

We look at how the company actually runs: where customers come in, what is done by hand, what gets dropped when everyone is busy. Out of that comes the list of where time, sales, information or money is being lost.

You leave with that list, whether we build anything or not
02

We design

We design the system around those problems, not the other way round. It fits the tools and the way of working you already have: what already works stays as it is.

You see what you will have before it exists

This is what we get asked to fix

If any of this sounds familiar, there is a system for it.

Opportunities come in and nobody follows them up in timeWe roll out a system that collects contacts from the website, from email and from ads, ranks them by interest and starts the follow-up on its own, with an alert when one has been sitting for days. Getting paid depends on somebody rememberingWe roll out the invoicing, due-date and collections system: invoices go out when they should, reminders send themselves, and one screen says who owes what and since when. It is our own software and you can try it right below. Customers wait too long for someone to replyWe roll out an agent that answers on the website and by email with your business's real information, sorts what comes in, and hands a person what needs a person. Projects slip and nobody sees it comingWe roll out a system that, the moment the client says yes, sets the project up with its tasks, its owners and its dates, and warns before anything gets stuck. Everyone keeps the information somewhere differentWe roll out a single place where clients, documents, contracts and renewal dates live, each with its own reminder, visible to whoever needs it. Every morning the numbers have to be asked forWe roll out a screen that says each day what happened yesterday and what has to be decided today, computed on your own operation's data.

If your problem looks like one of these, we adapt the system. If it looks like none of them, we build the one that is missing: there is no catalogue to choose from. They are built with automations, AI agents and integrations.
