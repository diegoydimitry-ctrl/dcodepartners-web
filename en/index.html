How we build it

Four steps. At each one you know what you put in and what you walk away with.

01

We analyse

It starts with a 30-minute call and continues with a review: we follow the real path of a customer, an order and an invoice, and note every point where a person has to bridge the gap.

From you
Half an hour, then a couple of hours from whoever does that work, with real examples.
What you keep
The written list of where the time and the money leak out. It’s yours whether we build it or not.
If we see nothing worth doing, we tell you on that call
02

We design

We take the heaviest item and decide what goes with what: what the system does on its own, what a person keeps deciding and which of your tools it connects to.

From you
Telling us what comes first. The rules of your business are yours to set.
What you keep
Scope, timeline and price in writing before anything starts.
You see what you’ll get before it exists
03

We put it in

We build it and get it working piece by piece, inside the tools you already use and with your team involved. Nothing changes all at once.

From you
Minimal access and one person who checks it does what it should.
What you keep
The system running, with the documentation and access in your name. If we ever stop working together, it keeps running.
It runs in your operation, not in a demo
04

We measure and improve

The system keeps a record of what it does: what ran on its own, what failed and where a person is still bridging a gap. That’s what it gets corrected with, every month.

From you
Half an hour a month to look at it together.
What you keep
Numbers from your own system, not from a slide deck.
Every month, with your own numbers
