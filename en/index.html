How we work

We read your company, find where the money leaks out and build the system that stops it.

01

We analyse

We follow the real path of a customer, an order and an invoice, end to end, and write down every point where a person has to bridge a gap. Out of that comes the list of where the time, the sales and the money leak out — and how many times a month each one happens.

You leave with that list whether we build it or not
02

We design

We take the heaviest thing on that list and decide what goes with what: what can run on its own, what a person keeps deciding, and which of your tools it connects to. What already works stays as it is, and the rules of your business are set by you.

You see what you will get before it exists
03

We put it in

We build it, connect it to what you already use and leave it running inside the daily job, piece by piece and with your team in it. We do not hand over a manual for somebody to put into practice: we hand over something already in practice.

It runs in your operation, not in a demo
04

We measure and improve

The system leaves a trace of everything it does: what ran on its own, what failed, and where a person is still bridging a gap. That is what it gets corrected with — and when the company changes, the system changes with it instead of going stale.

It can be checked, not just believed
This is what we get asked to fix

If any of this sounds familiar, there is a system for it.

Opportunities come in and nobody follows them up in timeWe put in a system that collects contacts from the site, the inbox and your ads, ranks them by interest and starts the follow-up on its own, with a warning when one has been sitting for days. Getting paid depends on someone rememberingWe put in the invoices, due dates and collections system: invoices go out when they should, reminders send themselves and a panel says who owes what and since when. It is our own software and it is above for you to try. Clients wait too long for someone to replyWe put in an agent that answers on the site and by email with the real information about your business, sorts what comes in and passes on to a person what needs a person. Projects slip and nobody sees it comingWe put in a system that, as soon as the client says yes, sets the project up with its tasks, its owners and its dates, and warns before something gets stuck. Everyone keeps the information somewhere differentWe put in one single place where clients, documents, contracts and renewal dates live, each with its reminder and visible to whoever needs it. Every morning the numbers have to be asked forWe put in a panel that says every day what happened yesterday and what has to be decided today, worked out from your own operation.

If your problem looks like one of these, we adapt the system. If it looks like none of them, we build the one that is missing: there is no catalogue to choose from. They are built with automations, AI agents and integrations.

No promises

This is exactly what happens, from the first call.

We have no guarantees to show you and no percentages to promise. What we can do is tell you the whole process and put the product in front of you: both can be checked before you sign anything.

  1. 01
    The first call 30 minutes
    What happens
    You tell us how you work. We ask about the path of a customer, an order and an invoice.
    What it takes
    Half an hour of yours, with nothing prepared.
    What you keep
    If we see nothing worth automating, we say so on that call.
  2. 02
    The review
    What happens
    We look at how you actually work: who does what, with which tool, and how many times a month.
    What it takes
    A couple of hours from the people who do that work, and real examples: an invoice, a quote, one of the usual emails.
    What you keep
    The written list of where the time and the money leak out, with how often each one happens. It is yours whether we build it or not.
  3. 03
    The proposal
    What happens
    We pick the heaviest one and show you what would be built: what runs on its own, what a person keeps deciding, and what it connects to.
    What it takes
    That you say what comes first. We do not invent the rules of your business.
    What you keep
    Scope, timeline and price in writing before anything starts.
  4. 04
    Putting it in
    What happens
    It gets built and put to work piece by piece, inside the tools you already use. Nothing changes all at once.
    What it takes
    The minimum access to what you already use, and one person on your side who checks it does what it should.
    What you keep
    The system running with your team using it, not a manual for switching it on.
  5. 05
    Control
    What happens
    It is written down: what each piece does, where it runs, who can change it and what happens if it fails one day.
    What it takes
    Nothing.
    What you keep
    The access and the documentation in your name. If we stop working together one day, it keeps running.
  6. 06
    The measure
    What happens
    The system records what it does: what ran on its own, what failed and where somebody is still bridging a gap.
    What it takes
    Half an hour a month to look at it together.
    What you keep
    Numbers out of your own system. You do not have to believe us: you check.
