01Today

Today every part of your company goes its own way.

People, processes, data and tools all work, but each one pulls in its own direction, like these four strands. For information to get from one to another, someone has to carry it by hand.

  • An order is entered in one program and typed again into another.
  • An invoice waits in an email until someone sees it.
  • The monthly report is put together by hand with data from three places.

05D-Code Finance

Finance reads your invoices and answers with your numbers.

Each pin in this lock is one piece of data on the invoice: the supplier, the amount, the VAT and the due date. Hand it an invoice: Finance reads it, records it, schedules the payment and notifies management. Then you can ask about your payments, your collections and your cash flow.

Change the figure and try it with your own. The key goes in, the four pieces of data fit and the invoice is recorded.
