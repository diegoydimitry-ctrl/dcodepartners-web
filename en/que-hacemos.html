What we do

We do not sell loose tools.
We build the system.

We look at where your time and money leak out, and build what stops it. Five pieces, combined as needed.

01

Custom systems

The system your company runs on, built to fit the way you actually work.

What it includes
  • We follow a customer, an order and an invoice end to end and measure where a person is acting as a bridge.
  • Scope, deadline and price in writing before anything starts.
  • Built in parts, inside the tools you already use.
  • It ends up running, with documentation and access in your name.
See it in detail

02

Automations

What is done by hand every week runs on its own, and tells you if something fails.

What it includes
  • One task, a whole process, or an AI flow that reads and classifies.
  • Error handling and an alert when something goes wrong.
  • Maintenance is separate and optional: with nobody behind it, it breaks the day someone changes their API.
See it in detail

03

AI agents

They answer with your business data and escalate to a person when it matters.

What it includes
  • Web, WhatsApp or phone; one channel or several at once.
  • They answer from your data, not from what they make up.
  • Everything they say is logged and reviewable.
  • Flat fee: you do not pay per conversation.
See it in detail

04

Integrations

Your CRM, ERP, site and spreadsheets talking to each other, with minimum permissions.

What it includes
  • Data is entered once and shows up where it should.
  • Also with your long-standing system, without touching its insides.
  • Error handling, retries and a trail of what happened.
See it in detail

05

Websites

We build your site, rebuild the one you have and connect it to your systems.

What it includes
  • A new site: design, copy, speed and looking right on a phone.

  • Connected: the form lands in your CRM, stock comes from the warehouse, the booking enters the diary.
  • With a chatbot if you want one, answering from your data.

07

Where to start

The same system, seen from where you work.

Each area is a way in, not a separate product. You start with the one that weighs most.

SalesNo deal lost because nobody followed upMarketingLeads that arrive sorted, not rawCustomerCustomers looked after even when their usual contact is awayOperationsJobs that start themselves and flag a delay before it happensFinanceInvoice and get paid without chasing anyoneSupportFast replies, even out of hoursAdministrationPaperwork in its place, without having to look for itLeadershipOne clear decision every morning, on real numbers
How we do it The method, in nine steps
  1. 01

    Your company

    Several areas, each with its own software, spreadsheets and WhatsApp. They all work. None of them talk to each other.

  2. 02

    Problems

    Work gets lost between areas: data copied by hand, forgotten follow-ups, three versions of the same customer.

  3. 03

    Analysis

    We follow a customer, an order and an invoice end to end, and measure how often a person acts as a bridge.

  4. 04

    Design

    Before building we decide what goes with what: what the system does alone and what a person still decides.

  5. 05

    Connection

    One piece of data for every area. The customer sales bring in is the one support looks after and finance invoices.

  6. 06

    Automation

    What one area finishes triggers the next: quote accepted, job scheduled, invoice issued. Without typing anything twice.

  7. 07

    AI

    AI reads emails and documents, sorts what comes in and proposes the reply. What matters is decided by a person.

  8. 08

    Measurement

    Everything leaves a trail: hours by hand, response times, tasks with no owner. Figures, not impressions.

  9. 09

    Continuous improvement

    Every month we look at the numbers and adjust. When your company changes, the system changes with it.
