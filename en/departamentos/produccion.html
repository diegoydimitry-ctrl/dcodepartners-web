Delivery — Smart Department

The Department that gets your projects launched on their own and delivered on time.

What it is

Delivery is the Department that turns every closed sale into a project under control from day one.

As soon as a client is won, Delivery creates the project, assigns its tasks based on your team's real workload, and tracks progress and the delivery date from start to finish — without anyone having to organize it by hand.
