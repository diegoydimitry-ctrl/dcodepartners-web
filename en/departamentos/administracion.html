Administration

Your company watched over, protected and in order.

Administration watches over the rest of the system to keep it running, protects the structure of your documents, and runs periodic backups of critical data.

Today it's the infrastructure we use to watch over ourselves; extending it to a client's own systems is a concrete configuration, not something already deployed outside D-Code.

One part of the system we build when your company needs it: adapted to how you work, not a closed package.

  • No one notices when something internal stops working until someone complains.
