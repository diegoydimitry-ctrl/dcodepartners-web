Operations — Smart Department

The Department that keeps your company protected and organized, effortlessly.

Operations watches over the rest of the system to keep it running, protects the structure of your documents, and runs periodic backups of critical data.

Today it's the infrastructure we use to watch over ourselves; extending it to a client's own systems is a concrete configuration, not something already deployed outside D-Code.

Failures caught within hours Documentation always organized Backups always up to date
What it is

Operations is the Department that protects how all the others run.

It watches over the rest of your company to keep everything running normally, keeps the folder structure for clients and projects organized, and stores periodic backups of critical operational data.
