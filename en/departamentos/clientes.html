Customer

Every client looked after, without depending on any one person.

Customer onboards, welcomes, and supports every client for the entire life of the relationship — with the same quality, no matter who on the team is on duty that day or whether anyone remembers to do it.

One part of the system we build when your company needs it: adapted to how you work, not a closed package.

Problems it eliminates

Stop improvising the welcome and finding out about renewals too late.

Today, without a system
  • The welcome varies depending on who handles it that day.
  • Onboarding is improvised client by client.
  • Renewals get discovered late, or not at all.
  • No one checks if the client is happy until they've already left.
With the system
  • Every new client gets the same welcome, always.
  • The onboarding plan is generated automatically for each client.
  • Renewals are tracked with enough lead time to act.
  • Satisfaction is measured at regular intervals, not just at the end.
