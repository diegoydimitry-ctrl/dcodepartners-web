Sales

We automate your sales so none is lost to poor follow-up.

Sales centralizes every opportunity, generates proposals in minutes, and flags what needs attention each morning — so your pipeline stops living inside one person's head.

One part of the system we build when your company needs it: adapted to how you work, not a closed package.

Try it running: Sales, with made-up data

01

Stop relying on one person's memory, or a spreadsheet.

Today, without a system

  • Sales depend on one person's memory.
  • There's no systematic prospecting, only what shows up by chance.
  • Proposals take days to go out.
  • Follow-up gets forgotten if nobody remembers to do it.

With the system

  • Every opportunity is centralized in one place, always up to date.
  • New opportunities are detected by sector, every day.
  • Proposals are generated in minutes, verified.
  • Nothing goes without follow-up: the team knows every morning.

02

How it works

A radar that finds opportunities, not a list you have to review.

A simplified view of the system — not data from a real client.

radar_sales.demo

DEMO

The radar sweeps the sector every day. Hover — or move keyboard focus — over a highlighted point or a stage to see how they match up.

03

In sequence

Marketing captures. Sales closes. And what closes keeps moving on its own.

Marketing captures and prioritizes

Every contact that reaches the website is qualified in real time, before it reaches the sales team.

Sales receives the opportunity ready to go

Without having to re-qualify anything, the team knows if it's a priority and adds it to the pipeline.

It closes, and the data keeps moving on its own

As soon as a proposal is accepted, that information is never re-typed: it automatically kicks off Customer and Operations.

04

The result

What your company gets.

No opportunity gets lost

Follow-up no longer depends on someone remembering: nothing goes unattended because it was forgotten.

Proposals in minutes, not days

Every proposal is generated from the prospect's real data, ready to send.

The team knows what's due today

Every morning it's clear which leads and which proposals need attention.

The pipeline stops living in one head

Every opportunity is centralized and visible, not scattered across notes and memory.

All you need is your services catalog and a proposal template. If you also want active prospecting, just tell us the sector and geographic area you're interested in. In the first meeting, we'll tell you what we would build and with what scope.
