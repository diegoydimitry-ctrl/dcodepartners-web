Sales — Smart Department

The Department that makes sure no sale is ever lost to poor follow-up.

Sales centralizes every opportunity, generates proposals in minutes, and flags what needs attention each morning — so your pipeline stops living inside one person's head.

Pipeline always centralized Proposals in minutes Nothing gets forgotten
What it is

Sales is the Department that makes sure no sales opportunity is ever lost to poor follow-up.

Instead of relying on one person's memory or a spreadsheet nobody updates, Sales centralizes every opportunity, keeps it alive, and flags each morning what needs attention — including new ones that arrive on their own, by sector, every day.

How it works with Marketing

Marketing captures. Sales closes. And what closes keeps moving on its own.

01

Marketing captures and prioritizes

Every contact that reaches the website is qualified in real time, before it reaches the sales team.

02

Sales receives the opportunity ready to go

Without having to re-qualify anything, the team knows if it's a priority and adds it to the pipeline.

03

It closes, and the data keeps moving on its own

As soon as a proposal is accepted, that information is never re-typed: it automatically kicks off Customer and Delivery.

Which companies get the most value

If your sales follow-up lives in someone's head or a spreadsheet, this is for you.

Sales delivers the most impact for sales teams of 1 to 8 people who don't yet have a documented process, and who currently track follow-up by hand or in a spreadsheet.
