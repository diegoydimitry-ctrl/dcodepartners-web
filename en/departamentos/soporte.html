Support AI — Smart Department

The Department that responds fast, even after hours.

Support AI turns every support email into a prioritized ticket with an automatically calculated SLA, complemented by a chat available 24 hours a day — today in security review before its access to client data is expanded.

TICKETS AI — LIVE CHAT AI — SECURITY REVIEW PENDING

Why we separate Tickets from Chat

Tickets AI works without caveats: every email becomes a prioritized ticket, without accessing any data beyond that email itself. The conversational chat, on the other hand, queries project and proposal data — and before we expand that access to more clients, we have a pending security review of how that per-client lookup is restricted. We'd rather say so now than sell it as finished.

What it is

Support AI is the Department that prioritizes every inquiry and responds even when your team is offline.

Every support email becomes a ticket with an automatically calculated priority and response deadline. In parallel, a chat available 24 hours a day will be able to respond using data from your projects and proposals once its security review is closed, and escalates to a ticket whenever it doesn't have enough information to answer with confidence.
