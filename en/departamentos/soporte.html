Support

We automate support to reply fast, even after hours.

Support turns every support email into a prioritized ticket with an automatically calculated SLA, complemented by a chat available 24 hours a day — today in security review before its access to client data is expanded.

One part of the system we build when your company needs it: adapted to how you work, not a closed package.

Try it running: Customer service, with made-up data

01

No more inbox that nobody knows where to start reading.

Today, without a system

  • Support emails arrive with no priority and no deadline.
  • Nobody knows which inquiry is urgent and which can wait.
  • Outside business hours, there's no coverage at all.
  • The team feels overwhelmed by the inbox.

With the system

  • Every email becomes a ticket with priority and SLA.
  • Real urgencies stand out without anyone reading every message.
  • The chat responds 24 hours a day, even after hours.
  • The team stops feeling overwhelmed by the inbox.
