Finance

Get paid sooner and see spend every day, not at month-end.

It invoices from the accepted quote, chases the collections and watches the spend — on D-Code Finance, the system we already use for our own accounts.

One part of the system we build when your company needs it: adapted to how you work, not a closed package.

What problems it eliminates — already, inside D-Code

You'll stop discovering unpaid invoices late, and spend at month-end.

Today, without a system
  • Invoices are generated by hand, one at a time.
  • Unpaid invoices are discovered late, or never.
  • Collection reminders depend on someone remembering.
  • Internal spend is only known at month-end.
With the system
  • Invoices are generated on their own from accepted quotes.
  • Collections speed up with reminders that escalate on their own.
  • Prolonged unpaid invoices are detected and flagged without being forgotten.
  • Spend is tracked day by day, with budget alerts.
What it is

Finance is designed to cover both halves of your financial picture: what you collect and what you spend.

It generates each invoice and tracks it until it is paid, escalating reminders if there is no response. Invoicing, collections, expenses and dashboard are one circuit.

In sequence

The moment there's something to invoice, Finance will know.

The project or client already exists

Operations and Customer launch the project and set up the client.

Finance generates and sends the invoice automatically

The moment a quote is accepted or a project is delivered, Finance generates the invoice on its own, without anyone having to ask.

Collection is tracked until it completes

Reminders escalate automatically if there is no response, without anyone having to keep watch.
