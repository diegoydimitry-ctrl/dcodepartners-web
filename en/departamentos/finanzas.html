What it is

Finance is designed to cover both halves of your financial picture: what you collect and what you spend.

The system generates each invoice and tracks its status until it's paid, automatically escalating reminders if there's no response. In parallel, it watches internal spend every day, not just when the month closes, and calculates a complete financial dashboard. Invoicing, collections, expenses and dashboard form a single circuit: each piece writes where the next one will read it.
