Finance — Smart Department

The Department designed to help you get paid sooner and track spend every day, not just at month-end.

Finance is designed to generate and send your invoices in minutes, escalate collection reminders until they're paid, and watch internal spend day by day — today still under construction, not yet a closed department.

COMING SOON Pieces already built Full circuit not yet closed

Why we're showing this but not selling it yet

Real pieces of Finance already exist — invoice generation, expense tracking, collection reminders — but they don't yet form a complete, end-to-end automatic circuit, and we won't present it as a closed department until it truly is one. What follows describes the planned design, not a service already in production.

What it is

Finance is designed to cover both halves of your financial picture: what you collect and what you spend.

The design generates each invoice document and tracks its status until it's paid, automatically escalating reminders if there's no response. In parallel, it watches internal spend every day, not just when the month closes. Some of these pieces are already built; the complete circuit is not yet.
