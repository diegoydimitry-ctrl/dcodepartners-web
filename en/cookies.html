Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar storage technologies dcodepartners.com uses, for what purpose, and how you can manage them, in compliance with Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and EU cookie regulations (Directive 2002/58/EC, as amended by Directive 2009/136/EC, and its implementation through the GDPR with regard to consent).

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in the user's browser when they visit it. Among other things, they make it possible to remember preferences, recognize the user on subsequent visits, or measure how the site is used. This same concept also covers other local device storage technologies (such as the browser's session storage), to which this policy also applies.

2. Consent management

When you access the Website, a cookie consent panel is shown that allows you to accept, reject, or granularly configure non-essential cookies. This panel is managed through CookieYes, an independent consent management platform (CMP), and its configuration is not part of this document nor altered by it. Cookies that are strictly necessary for the Website to function are always installed, as they are exempt from consent under Article 22.2 LSSI-CE; all others are only activated if the user expressly accepts them.

D-Code Partners does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies, whether its own or from third parties, for personalized advertising purposes.

4. Managing cookies from your browser

In addition to the consent panel, users can at any time allow, block, or delete cookies installed on their device by configuring their browser's settings. How to do this depends on each browser; you can consult the official help pages for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge for more information. Please note that blocking certain technical cookies may affect the proper functioning of some parts of the Website, such as the contact form.

5. More information

For any questions about this Cookie Policy, or about the associated processing of personal data, you can write to us at dcodedepartment@gmail.com. For information about the processing of personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
