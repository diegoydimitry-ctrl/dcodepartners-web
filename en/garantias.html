Guarantees and process

What we guarantee.
In writing, before we start.

We do not promise a result before we have measured it with you. What we do guarantee is how we work — and that you can hold us to from day one.

The commitments

Five things that do not depend on how the project goes.

01

You will know what we are going to build before we build it.

The scope, the tools it connects to and what we will measure are agreed with you. Without that agreement, we do not start.

02

No lock-in.

There is no minimum term and no cancellation penalty. If you decide to stop, you stop.

03

Confidentiality.

We access only what is strictly necessary for each process. If your company needs a signed confidentiality agreement before we start, we sign it — you only have to ask.

04

The system is yours.

The systems we deploy into your operation belong to you. Our job is to design them, build them and leave them running.

05

Your tools, not ours.

We design to integrate with what you already use — CRM, spreadsheets, ERP or website — instead of forcing you to replace it.
