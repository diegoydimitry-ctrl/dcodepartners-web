The technical layer · AI Automation

Processes that run themselves, 24 hours a day.

It's the engine that runs inside every Smart Department. We design systems that take on your business's repetitive operational tasks: the ones that eat up your team's hours but don't require their judgment at every step.

What we automate

Real examples of processes that stop depending on one person.

Document management

Classification, data extraction, and filing of invoices, contracts, or forms, without manual intervention.

Reporting and tracking

Periodic reports generated and sent automatically from your data, instead of gathering them by hand every week.

Client onboarding

Automatic welcome sequences, data collection, and account activation from the first point of contact.

Sales follow-up

Reminders, status updates, and automatic alerts so no prospective client goes unanswered.

Invoicing and payments

Invoice generation, payment reminders, and basic reconciliation without reviewing every transaction by hand.

Recurring tasks

Any process someone on your team currently repeats every day, week, or month, with clear, repeatable rules.

How we do it

Automation built on your real operations, not a mock-up.

We start by identifying the highest-impact processes during the initial diagnosis. Then we design the workflow, connect it to your current tools, and deploy it directly into your operations, within the free trial month.

You no longer depend on someone "remembering" to run the process: the system does it by default, and a person only steps in when their judgment truly adds value.
