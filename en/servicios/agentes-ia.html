The technical layer · AI Agents

Customer service and sales that make no one wait.

Not a standalone chatbot: it's the conversation piece inside your Support or Sales Department, coordinated with the rest of the system. It responds in your website's chat and by email with your business's tone and information, and knows when to hand the conversation over to a person.

What they solve

Every inquiry handled instantly, on the channel your customer prefers.

Immediate first contact

Answers frequently asked questions, hours, prices, and availability in seconds, any time of day.

Lead qualification

Captures the key information from every contact and has it ready in your CRM before you even speak with that person.

First-line support

Resolves your existing customers' most common queries without them having to wait for someone to be free.

Escalation to your team

When a conversation needs human judgment, the agent hands it off with all the context already gathered.

Reactivation and follow-up

Automatic follow-up messages for appointments, pending quotes, or abandoned carts.

Your brand's tone and data

The agent responds only with the information you give it, in your company's tone, without making up answers.

How we do it

An agent trained on your information, not on generic answers.

We start from your FAQs, your catalog, and how you communicate. Today we deploy the agent on web chat and email; other channels are evaluated case by case — we don't sell them as available by default. It stays supervised during the trial month so we can tune its behavior against real cases.

It's a piece that combines especially well with Integrations, so every conversation gets logged directly in your CRM.
