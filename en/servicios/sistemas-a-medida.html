What we do

When what you need cannot be bought ready-made.

We look at how your company actually works — following a customer, an order and an invoice end to end — and build the system that does that work. Not one more tool: the place everything goes through.

  • It starts with a measurement

    How many times a person acts as a bridge between two programs. That figure decides what gets built first.

  • Scope and price before anything is touched

    In writing. If it changes along the way, it gets rewritten.

  • In parts

    The first piece works in weeks, not months. What comes next is decided with it already running.

  • Yours when it is done

    The code, the data and the access, in your name. No lock-in.
