The technical layer · Integrations

Your tools, actually connected to each other.

It's what makes your Smart Departments share one data source instead of living in isolation. We link your CRM, your ERP, your website, and the spreadsheets you already use so information travels on its own, without exporting, copying, or pasting anything by hand.

How we do it

The glue that makes the rest of the system work.

Most automation systems or AI agents only reach their full potential when connected to the tools you already use. That's why integrations almost never stand alone: they accompany the other two services so information flows from end to end.

During the diagnosis, we review which tools you use today and which connections have the greatest impact before touching anything.

Is this for your business?

If you already use several digital tools but pass information from one to another by hand, this service is usually the one with the most immediate impact.

If you don't yet have established digital tools, it may be worth talking about AI Automation first, to define the process before connecting it.

What we connect

Data is generated once and reaches every place you need it.

CRM and lead capture

Every form, chat, or call enters your CRM directly, without dumping it in by hand at the end of the day.

Spreadsheets and BI

Your dashboards update themselves from real data, not from a weekly manual export.

ERP and invoicing

Orders, quotes, and invoices synced between your website, your ERP, and your invoicing tool.

Internal communication

Automatic alerts by email or in the tool your team already uses (with API access) whenever something happens that they need to know about.

APIs and in-house tools

If your company uses custom software, we can still connect it through its API when one is available.

Security built in

Every integration uses minimal permissions and its own credentials, never generic access to your entire system.
