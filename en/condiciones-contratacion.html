Terms of Service

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

This document is new relative to the site's previous legal documentation: no page previously governed the terms under which D-Code Partners provides its services to clients. It is being added now because D-Code Partners is about to start working with real clients, and an automation/technology consulting service without published terms of service leaves essential matters unregulated: scope of service, ownership of what is developed, limits of liability, and cancellation terms. These Terms of Service govern the relationship between D-Code Partners and its clients (companies or professionals) for the provision of business process automation, artificial intelligence, systems integration, and technology consulting services.

1. Parties

These terms apply to the relationship between D-Code Partners (identified in the Legal Notice) and the client contracting its services, with the specific relationship detailed in the commercial proposal or quote accepted by the client for each project.

2. Purpose and scope of the service

D-Code Partners provides services for the design, development, and implementation of business process automations, AI-based agents and integrations, and integration of the client's tools and systems (indicatively: n8n, Airtable, Notion, Google Workspace, CRM, and the client's own business APIs). The specific scope of each project (features, timelines, deliverables, and price) is defined in the specific proposal or quote accepted by the client, which forms an integral part of these terms. Any feature not expressly included in the proposal will be considered out of scope and may give rise to an additional proposal.

3. Trial period / free month

When D-Code Partners offers a trial period or a month of service at no cost, its specific terms (duration, scope included, and what happens once it ends) will be expressly communicated to the client before the service begins. Unless otherwise stated in the specific communication, continuation of the service after that period will require the client's express acceptance of a commercial proposal; under no circumstances will recurring billing begin automatically without prior notice to the client.

4. Price and payment terms

The price of each project will be as stated in the proposal or quote accepted by the client, and will include applicable taxes under current legislation. Payment terms (deposits, milestones, recurring billing in the case of maintenance services) will likewise be specified in that proposal.   • Amounts paid in advance corresponding to work not started or not performed will be refunded to the client.
  • For maintenance or recurring subscription services, cancellation will take effect at the end of the current billing period, with no new charges arising from that point onward.

D-Code Partners may terminate the contract in the event of repeated non-payment by the client, following notice, without prejudice to claiming any amounts owed.

Unless otherwise agreed in the commercial proposal, once the project price has been paid in full, the client acquires ownership of the automations, workflows, and configurations developed specifically for it. D-Code Partners retains ownership of its own methods, internal templates, prior know-how, and generic reusable components that are not specific to the client's project, and may reuse them in other projects provided this does not involve disclosing the client's confidential information.
