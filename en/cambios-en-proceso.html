Changes in Progress

We don't just talk about AI. Here's what we're building right now.

D-Code Partners isn't a finished system — it's one that evolves every week. Here you'll see what we're actually working on: what's built, what's under validation, and what's still in development. No invented dates, no dressed-up status.

The main project

D-Code OS: the single panel a company is run from.

The Intelligent Departments already do the work. D-Code OS is where all that work becomes visible, understandable and manageable — without opening five different tools.

Today's problem

A company's information lives scattered.

  • Clients, in a spreadsheet
  • Invoices, in another program
  • Projects, in someone's head
  • Issues, buried in email
  • And the automations, on their own

Nobody has the full picture. And when something stops working, it's found out late.

What we're building

One place where everything is connected.

How it works, in four steps

  1. 01 The Departments work Each Intelligent Department does its daily job: it detects, decides, alerts and acts.
  2. 02 Everything is recorded Every action leaves a trace: what happened, when, and with what result. Nothing depends on someone writing it down.
  3. 03 D-Code OS brings it together That trace is organised into a single panel, per company, with each client's data kept separate from the rest.
  4. 04 You steer You see the real state of your company and you decide. The system informs; the important calls stay yours.

Also under way

Other fronts open right now.

finance.evolution UNDER VALIDATION

D-Code Finance, next stage

What we're improving — The full Finance circuit: automatic invoicing from accepted quotes, payment reminders that escalate on their own, daily expense control, a financial dashboard, and an assistant that answers questions about the real data.

Current status — The circuit is built and running end to end in controlled tests. It has not yet issued its first real invoice: that is exactly the step we're taking now, and until it happens we don't offer it as a finished Department.

Why it matters — It's the Department with the easiest return to prove in euros: payments that arrive sooner, spending that doesn't surprise you at month end.

single.identity UNDER WAY

One record per company

What we're building — A layer that recognises when the same company shows up in several places at once — as an opportunity, as a client and as a project — and brings it together into a single record, instead of treating it as three different companies.

Current status — Running and updating daily against our own data. It's the groundwork that will let D-Code OS show a whole company at a glance instead of scattered fragments.

Why it matters — Without it, a single panel shows the same company repeated. It's unglamorous work that decides whether everything else makes sense.

In production

Automatic commercial proposals

When a priority opportunity comes in, the proposal is prepared on its own and a person reviews it before it goes out. Audited against what actually runs, not just against what was published.

Active

External communication guardrail

No automation can write to anyone outside without passing a prior check: if explicit authorisation is missing, the send is blocked by default. It lets us build fast without risking anyone's trust.

Active

Watching our own system

Periodic backups and automatic alerts when an automation fails. We'd rather find out ourselves than have the client find out first.
