Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

At D-Code Partners we respect the privacy of the people who visit this Website or get in touch with us, and we process personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and the guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD), and other applicable Spanish and EU legislation.

  • Contact email: dcodedepartment@gmail.com
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): appointing a DPO is not mandatory under Article 34 LOPDGDD, given the volume and nature of the processing carried out. Any data protection query may be addressed to the contact email above.

2. Data we collect and how

We collect personal data only in the following cases:

  • Contact form ("Book a Call"): full name, company, email, phone (optional), and the content of the message sent.
  • Website AI assistant (chat): the content of the messages the user voluntarily types in the conversation. The conversation history is stored temporarily in the user's own browser (session storage) and is deleted when the tab is closed; it is not automatically linked to an identity unless the user themselves provides identifying details within the conversation.
  • Browsing: technical data automatically collected for analytics and security purposes (see Cookie Policy), including the IP address, which is processed transiently for security reasons (limiting abusive requests to the form and to the AI assistant).

We do not collect special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR) or data belonging to minors. The Website is not directed at children under 14.

PurposeLawful basis (Art. 6 GDPR)

Audience measurement and Website analyticsUser's prior consent given through the cookie panel (Art. 6.1.a)

4. Automated decisions

As part of our internal processes, we use artificial intelligence tools to support the internal classification and prioritization of the commercial requests we receive (for example, an indicative interest score). This score is solely a support tool for our team: no decision that produces legal effects or significantly affects you is made in a wholly automated manner, within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR — every relevant commercial decision (for example, accepting or not accepting a proposal) is always made by a member of our team.

5. Recipients and data processors

In order to provide our services, some data is shared with providers acting as data processors (Art. 28 GDPR), who process the data solely on our instructions. The providers we actually use are:

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting of the Website and of the functions that process the contact form.
  • n8n (n8n Cloud) — automation engine to which contact form data is sent for internal processing and routing (classifying the request, logging, and notifying the team).
  • Airtable, Inc. — operational database where commercial requests, clients, and projects are recorded and managed.
  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Workspace: Gmail, Google Drive) — corporate email and storage of documents related to the commercial or contractual relationship.
  • Notion Labs, Inc. — internal documentation of business processes and decisions (internal use, not client-facing).
  • Google Ireland Limited (Gemini API) and Anthropic, PBC (Claude API) — providers of the artificial intelligence model that generates the responses of the Website's conversational assistant; they receive the content of the message written by the user and the recent conversation history in order to generate a response.
  • Resend — sending transactional confirmation emails when the primary channel for sending the form is unavailable (backup route).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — protection of the contact form against automated submissions (Cloudflare Turnstile) and, separately, network protection for the Website.
  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics) — audience measurement, only if the user has given consent through the cookie panel.

We do not sell or transfer personal data to third parties for advertising purposes. There are no other data transfers except where required by law.

6. International transfers

Some of the providers listed above (Anthropic, Resend, and certain Google and n8n Cloud services depending on their regional configuration) may involve transfers of data outside the European Economic Area, in particular to the United States. In such cases, the transfer is carried out under the safeguards provided for in Chapter V of the GDPR, in particular through the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or another adequacy mechanism offered by the relevant provider.

7. Retention period

Data from contact requests that do not result in a commercial relationship will be retained for a maximum of 24 months from the last contact, after which it will be deleted or anonymized. Data of clients with an active contractual relationship will be retained for as long as that relationship remains in force and, thereafter, for the applicable statutory limitation periods (tax, commercial). The AI assistant history stored in the browser is automatically deleted when the session is closed.

8. Rights of data subjects

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing, and data portability at any time, as well as withdraw any consent given at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal), by writing to:

dcodedepartment@gmail.com, indicating which right you wish to exercise and enclosing a copy of a document proving your identity.

If you consider that the processing of your data does not comply with applicable regulations, you have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)www.aepd.es.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration, including restricted access to the systems that hold personal data (limited to Diego Siñeriz and Dimitry Sosenko), the use of providers that offer encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and authentication on their platforms, and minimizing the data requested to what is strictly necessary for each purpose. You can review the full details in our Information Security Policy.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the law or in the services or providers used by D-Code Partners. The date of the last update appears at the top of this document.
