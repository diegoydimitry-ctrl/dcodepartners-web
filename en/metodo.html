The D-Code Method

Understand first.
Then build.

We do not start by writing code. We start by looking at how your company actually works, and we build nothing until you know exactly what we are going to build.

The journey

From the first call to a system that runs.

01

Diagnosis

We walk through your real operation: which processes eat the most hours, where opportunities are lost, and which repetitive tasks slow the team down. Not a questionnaire — a reading of how you work.

02

Design and scope

Out of that comes a concrete proposal: what gets automated, what a person keeps deciding, which tools it connects to and what we are going to measure. Agreed before anything is built.

03

Build

We adapt the pieces we already know how to build and develop whatever your case needs that does not exist yet. It is deployed into your real operation, not an isolated sandbox.

04

Measure and adjust

We review with concrete data — hours, response times, processes automated — against your starting point. And we adjust whatever needs adjusting.

Before anything is built, you will know exactly what we are going to build.

The scope, the tools it connects to and what we will measure are agreed with you. It is the part of the work almost nobody shows, and it is the part that decides whether the rest goes well.

What we do not do

Three things we rule out from the start.

  • We do not force you to change tools. We design to integrate with your CRM, your spreadsheets or your ERP, rather than replace them.
  • We do not hand over a black box. If a system cannot be explained in five minutes, we are probably overcomplicating it.
  • We do not sell a closed catalogue. The eight Departments are pieces we already know how to build. If your problem asks for something else, we build something else.
