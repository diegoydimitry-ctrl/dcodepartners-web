The D-Code Method

We start with facts, not promises.

The market is full of consultancies that sell smoke before proving anything. We work the other way around: we implement first, measure the real impact, and only then talk about price.

0% RISK No lock-in No fine print
The D-Code Protocol — four phases

From the first call to results on the table.

01

Diagnosis and design of your custom system

We analyze how your business actually runs: which processes eat up the most hours, where leads slip through the cracks, and what repetitive tasks are holding your team back. From there we design an automation system built specifically for your business — not a generic template.

02

Implementation in your real operations (weeks 1–4)

We deploy the systems directly into your day-to-day operations, not in an isolated test environment. We supervise them closely throughout the month, adjusting whatever's needed along the way.

03

Results review (day 30)

We sit down with you and go through concrete data: hours saved, response times, processes automated. No estimates, no projections — results that have already happened.

04

We agree on the value of the service

Only then do we talk numbers, with the real impact already proven. If the results aren't there, there's no invoice. If you decide to continue, you do it knowing exactly what you're gaining.

Why we work this way

Trust is proven, not declared.

We're a young company. Rather than make up for that with grand promises or inflated case studies, we'd rather let the implementation itself speak for us during that first month.

It's a model that puts the obligation on us, not you: if the system doesn't work, we don't get paid. That aligns our incentives with yours from day one.

No proven benefit, no invoice. As simple as that.
