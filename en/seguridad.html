Information Security Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

This document is new relative to the site's previous legal documentation. It is being added because, as we begin working with real clients who will entrust D-Code Partners with access to their own systems (CRM, email, automations), it is standard professional practice and a necessary sign of trust to honestly explain what security measures are actually in place — without claiming certifications or measures that D-Code Partners does not yet have.

1. Scope

This policy describes the technical and organizational measures that D-Code Partners currently applies to protect its own information and that of its users and clients, in the course of its automation and artificial intelligence activities.

2. Data minimization principle

D-Code Partners requests and stores only the data strictly necessary to provide each service. We do not collect data "just in case," nor do we expand access to client systems beyond what is necessary for the contracted project.

3. Access control

Access to internal systems containing information about users, leads, or clients (Airtable, corporate Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, n8n Cloud) is limited to D-Code Partners' two principals: Diego Siñeriz and Dimitry Sosenko. Each platform used manages its own authentication; D-Code Partners does not store third-party passwords or client credentials outside of each tool's own credential managers (for example, n8n's encrypted credential store).

4. Encryption and data transmission

All communications between the user's browser and the Website, as well as with the backend services used (Vercel, n8n Cloud, Airtable, Google Workspace), take place over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No personal data is transmitted unencrypted between D-Code Partners' own systems.

5. Providers and subcontracting

D-Code Partners only works with technology providers of recognized standing in their field (among others: Vercel, Cloudflare, Google Workspace, n8n, Airtable, Notion, Anthropic), and reviews that such providers offer adequate contractual guarantees (terms of service, and where applicable data processing agreements) before incorporating them into its operations. Details of which provider processes which type of personal data are set out in the Privacy Policy.

6. Backups

Critical operational information (client, project, and billing data) is backed up periodically through automated backups to cloud storage (Google Drive), with an internal notification for each backup performed.

7. Monitoring and incidents

D-Code Partners automatically monitors the proper functioning of its internal automation systems, generating alerts in the event of execution failures. Should a personal data breach occur that poses a risk to the rights and freedoms of the affected individuals, D-Code Partners will notify the Spanish Data Protection Agency within the 72-hour period set out in Article 33 GDPR and, where applicable under Article 34 GDPR, the affected individuals.

8. What we currently do NOT have

In the interest of transparency: D-Code Partners does not currently hold ISO 27001 certification or an Information Security Management System formally audited by a third party. The measures described in this document are those actually in place today; this policy will be reviewed and expanded as security requirements evolve.

9. Contact

Any question or communication relating to information security, including notification of a detected vulnerability, may be addressed to dcodedepartment@gmail.com.
